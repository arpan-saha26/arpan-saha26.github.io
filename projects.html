Master's Project

Indian Institute of Technology Madras

Supervisor: Dr. Bidhan Chandra Sardar · Jan 2026 – May 2026

    For my master’s project, I studied the variational framework for second-order elliptic boundary value problems, focusing on weak formulations and the role of boundary conditions. Research Experience

Students Undergraduate Research Graduate Excellence (SURGE) Program 2025

Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur

Supervisor: Dr. Malay Banerjee · May 2025 – July 2025

    As part of the SURGE programme at IIT Kanpur, I studied predator–prey dynamics in a two-patch environment, focusing on the effect of spatial redistribution on population stability. I studied a four-dimensional ODE model and, using fast–slow time-scale separation, derived a two-dimensional aggregated model. I further analysed its equilibria, stability, and degenerate Hopf bifurcation, supported by MATLAB simulations through phase portraits and time-series plots.

Topics: Predator–Prey Dynamics · Dynamical Systems · MATLAB
